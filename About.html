About us:

This website is proudly brought to you by an amazing non-profit organization named CodeMzansi, this organization is founded by Max Sekgabi, when it was all a dream back in 2022, Max travelled around the continent of Africa and realised the shortage and huge gap this continent had when it came to evolution and technology. As you know due to globalization technology is now at the fore front in today's world and economy, but however Africa's youth and teens are very outdated when it comes to technology and computing skills which is the main reason why this continent is stagnant. That gave Max a hyphothesis that how much would the youth and teens of Africa learn and prosper if they had basic computer skills and technology knowledge, so that made Max great an organization that would be determined to teach Africa's young brilliant minds about technology and computing skills, as he saw this as a massive opportunity to also reduce the unemployement rate which is drastically increasing per annum.

The mission and Vision:

The mission of this astonishing organization is clear and straight, our objective and goal is to better the youth the best way possible so how else could that be done if it isnt through education, as it is the best shield a individual can own at the moment, however this will be done through traveling around Africa and targeting less privilege communities and townships and going to them, educating the youth when it comes to technology because as you can see the evolution and globalization of the world needs any individual in the world especially the ones in the less priviledged countries to have the basic skills and knowledge of computer literacy, rest assured that our team teaches young people the basuc techniques of using a computer, such as the hardware of a computer and all it's components as how they function and how to fix them, shortcuts that can be done to make things easier for the computer and user, we also teach individuals how to make software to increase their credibility, critical thinking skills and intelligence.
