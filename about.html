About CodeMzansi:

This website is proudly brought to you by an amazing non-profit organization named CodeMzansi, this organization is founded by Max Sekgabi, when it was all a dream back in 2022, Max travelled around the continent of Africa and realised the shortage and huge gap this continent had when it came to evolution and technology. As you know due to globalization technology is now at the fore front in today's world and economy, but however Africa's youth and teens are very outdated when it comes to technology and computing skills which is the main reason why this continent is stagnant. That gave Max a hypothesis that how much would the youth and teens of Africa learn and prosper if they had basic computer skills and technology knowledge, so that made Max start an organization that would be determined to teach Africa's young brilliant minds about technology and computing skills, as he saw this as a massive opportunity to also reduce the unemployment rate which is drastically increasing per annum.

Our Mission:

To empower underprivileged youth in Africa with essential technology and computing skills, fostering innovation, critical thinking, and digital literacy to bridge the digital divide.

Our Vision:

To create a future where every young individual in Africa has access to quality technology education, enabling them to thrive in the digital age and contribute meaningfully to their communities and the global economy.

The mission and Vision:

The mission of this astonishing organization is clear and straight, our objective and goal is to better the youth the best way possible so how else could that be done if it isn't through education, as it is the best shield an individual can own at the moment, however this will be done through traveling around Africa and targeting less privileged communities and townships and going to them, educating the youth when it comes to technology because as you can see the evolution and globalization of the world needs any individual in the world especially the ones in the less privileged countries to have the basic skills and knowledge of computer literacy, rest assured that our team teaches young people the basic techniques of using a computer, such as the hardware of a computer and all it's components as how they function and how to fix them, shortcuts that can be done to make things easier for the computer and user, we also teach individuals how to make software to increase their credibility, critical thinking skills and intelligence.

The goal of this organization and website is to provide accessible technology education to underprivileged youth in Africa, empowering them with the skills needed to thrive in the digital age, this goal is clear and evident by the work we do, we try our best to teach these young generation about technology as it evolves everday and is steadily occupying every work field. Although our goal can be accomplished through sponsors cand organizations that support our goals and objectives, we want sponsors that see and believe in our vision as much as we do and understand that this organization is soon going to become one of their best investments ever done.
