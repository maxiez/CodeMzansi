Welcome to CodeMzansi, where we change the lives of many in order for you and us to prosper and become a success.

The focus and aim of this magnificent website is to further educate the society about the organization called CodeMzansi, this is a non-profit organization that travels around Africa in order to assist people with technology and offer basic skills and training free of charge around the continent of Africa as means to make our continent a better place, day by day.
